Scoped certificate for JSP-000255 (minimum distinct unit fractions to represent a rational).
The rational 4/5 is a sum of 3 distinct unit fractions:
  4/5 = 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/20   (denominators 2, 4, 20 pairwise distinct).
(Verified: 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/20 = 10/20 + 5/20 + 1/20 = 16/20 = 4/5.)
This gives an upper bound of 3 on the number of distinct unit fractions needed for 4/5.
