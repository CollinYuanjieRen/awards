Scoped results for JSP-000167 (sparse ruler problem; [ErGa48] Erdős–Gál 1948).
Record: minimum number of marks on an integer ruler to measure every length 1..N as a difference of
two marks, and the limiting ratio of this minimum to sqrt(N).

(1) Elementary counting lower bound: a mark set S has at most C(|S|,2) distinct positive differences,
so if it measures every length in {1..L}, then L <= C(|S|,2).

(2) Perfect-ruler certificate: the 4-mark ruler {0,1,4,6} measures every length 1..6:
    6-0=6, 6-1=5, 4-0=4, 4-1=3, 6-4=2, 1-0=1.
So its differences are exactly {1,2,3,4,5,6}. (C(4,2)=6=L, so it is a perfect ruler.)
(Both verified independently in Python.)
