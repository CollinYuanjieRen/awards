Scoped certificate for JSP-000265 (minimal nonempty zero-sum collection of signed integer reciprocals).
The collection of three signed unit fractions {1/2, -1/3, -1/6} (i.e. +1/2, -1/3, -1/6) sums to 0:
  1/2 - 1/3 - 1/6 = 3/6 - 2/6 - 1/6 = 0.
It is minimal: no nonempty proper subcollection sums to 0. The six proper nonempty subsums are
  1/2, -1/3, -1/6, 1/2-1/3 = 1/6, 1/2-1/6 = 1/3, -1/3-1/6 = -1/2,
all nonzero. (Verified in Python with exact rationals.)
