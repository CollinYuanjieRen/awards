Scoped lower-bound certificate for JSP-000685 (representations as a sum of two cubes;
[Ma35b] Mahler 1935; [St08]). Record question: how many representations can one integer have
as a sum of two integer cubes?
Certificate (positive-cube / taxicab reading): the Hardy-Ramanujan number 1729 has (at least)
two representations as a sum of two positive cubes:
  1729 = 1^3 + 12^3   (1 + 1728)
  1729 = 9^3 + 10^3   (729 + 1000)
The two unordered pairs {1,12} and {9,10} are distinct, so 1729 has at least two representations.
(Verified independently: 1+1728 = 729+1000 = 1729.)
